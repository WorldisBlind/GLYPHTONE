How to use GLYPHTONE

Grid

Tap a cell to place a note there. Tap it again to remove it. The playhead sweeps left to right as it plays.

Solo / Ensemble

Solo — one timbre per cell. Switching the selected instrument recolors your whole pattern instead of stacking a new sound on top.

Ensemble — layer multiple timbres in the same cell, one on top of another.

Transport

▶ Play / Stop. Tempo sets the BPM. Swing delays every other 16th note for a looser, groovier feel.

Generate

Builds a new phrase using the mode selected in the dropdown next to it: Melody, Up, Down, Up+Down, Down+Up, Zigzag, Wave, Bounce, Random, or Drum. Picking a new mode previews it instantly — press Generate again to reroll a fresh variation.

Undo reverts the last Generate or Clear — press it repeatedly to step back up to 5 times. Clear empties the grid you're currently viewing; hold it down instead of tapping to clear all 4 bars at once.

Scale & Octave

Scale changes the notes' pitches without moving where they're placed. Octave (±2) transposes everything up or down.

Song parts

Four bars (1 BAR–4 BAR) each hold their own pattern and auto-save as you edit. Tap a bar's name to view/edit it.

Tap the small 🔗 chain icon on the right of a bar to mark it for chained playback. With one or more bars chain-marked, playback cycles through just those bars in order, looping back to the first. With nothing chain-marked, playback simply loops whichever bar you're currently viewing.

Export

Export WAV renders the current pattern to an audio file. Export MIDI saves it as a Standard MIDI File you can import into a DAW.

Instruments

Tap an instrument to select it for placing notes. Tap the small fader icon on the right of each one to open a popup and adjust that instrument's volume.
